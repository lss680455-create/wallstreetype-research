# G7 组凝练：做空与尽职调查（Short-Selling & Forensic Due Diligence）

> **组总览 / Group Overview — 做空研究的方法论主线 (Methodology Mainline)**
>
> The ten reports share a common spine: they escalate *signals of fraud/overvaluation visible in public information* into *falsifiable hypotheses → independent evidence chains → adversarial public argument*, while explicitly managing the two true enemies of a short: timing mismatch and funding mismatch. The mainline has four stages:
>
> **(1) Signal discovery (信号发现)** — no insider access, only public documents: internal-consistency of financials (Chanos: profit–cash-flow divergence), whether cash can be physically verified (McCrum's escrow accounts), inverted incentive attribution (Burry studied lenders, not borrowers), and auditor-client size mismatch (Hindenburg on Shah Dhandharia).
>
> **(2) Independent verification (独立验证)** — external data management cannot rebut: field surveillance (Luckin store video), engineering facts vs narrative (Nikola's rolling-downhill truck), ownership and supply-chain penetration (Adani's Mauritius shells, SMCI's family suppliers), channel black-box audits (Valeant/Philidor).
>
> **(3) Adversarial argument (对抗性论证)** — every allegation independently verifiable; evidence pre-archived before publication (Adani Wayback); rebuttals answered point-by-point after the company responds (Nikola debate-style updates); research packaged into compounding assets (Einhorn's book, Soros's falsifiable theses).
>
> **(4) Survival management (存活管理)** — position, don't time (Soros); encode the conclusion into capped-loss contracts (Burry's CDS); recognize that "being right too early is functionally identical to being wrong."

### The Subprime Short: The 100 Data Set (Scion Capital, Michael Burry, 2005–2008)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Pure public-filing forensics — mined ~100 subprime mortgage-bond prospectuses from EDGAR, tabulating 2/28 ARM reset math, low-doc loan shares, and LTVs across successive vintages to document *structural drift* (each deal worse than the last). Inverted the incentive question: studied lenders/brokers (paid by volume, holding no risk after securitization) rather than borrowers.
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: Thesis rested on reset arithmetic — the loans were designed to fail regardless of house prices — not on default forecasting; conclusion encoded into the trade itself by buying CDS on specific deals (defined payoff, loss capped at premium, no borrow dependency).
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Vintage-cohort comparison tables, reset schedules, pool-composition breakdowns — comparative evidence, not decoration.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: Read deal documents, not headlines; track cohort drift across vintages; ask who gets paid by volume and who actually holds the risk.
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: Being right two years early was functionally equivalent to being wrong — premium bleed plus investor redemptions nearly killed Scion; thesis validity ≠ trade viability; the funding structure must survive the gap.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① Mine offering documents for early signals and compare cohort structures over time; ② encode short conclusions into capped-loss contracts (CDS/options); ③ budget for "right too early" — fund a 2+ year gap with a redemption-proof structure.

### Reflexivity: The Alchemy of Finance (Soros Fund Management, George Soros, 1987)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Reflexivity as a hypothesis engine — hunt markets where expectations feed back into fundamentals (credit/leverage-driven booms); map boom-bust sequence stages; price-vs-fundamentals divergence at "far-from-equilibrium" conditions is the signal.
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: A view = falsifiable hypothesis + position; the book openly documents his own wrong calls (e.g., 1987 crash) — credibility through disclosed failure; "it's not whether you're right or wrong but how much you make when right and lose when wrong."
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Conceptual reflexive-loop / boom-bust schematics rather than data charts — pattern templates for regime identification.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: Locate, don't time; predefine the falsification event; size positions so being wrong is survivable and being right is transformative.
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: Being early-and-right on overvaluation is functionally the same as being wrong; exit when the hypothesis breaks, not when price moves; in reflexive markets, fundamentals are not a stable anchor.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① Every thesis must carry an explicit "what would falsify this" trigger; ② express views through position size, not timing predictions; ③ exit on hypothesis breakage — never hold against evidence out of price conviction.

### Adani Group: The Largest Con in Corporate History (Hindenburg Research, Nathan Anderson, 2023)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Two-year investigation; reconstructed the group's ownership from public filings across dozens of jurisdictions, tracing flows from Vinod Adani–controlled Mauritius shells (DRI records: ~$808M skimmed, ~$900M routed to Mauritius) back into listed entities via total return swaps; auditor-fit red flag (tiny firm Shah Dhandharia auditing a giant conglomerate); key preservation move — archived the auditor's website via the Wayback Machine before it was taken down.
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: Roughly seven allegation clusters, each independently verifiable with cited documents; ended with 88 unanswered questions; a separate valuation leg (85%+ downside vs peers) kept independent from the fraud claims.
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Shell-network org charts, fund-flow diagrams, annotated document scans, satellite imagery — documents as evidence.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: Ownership-penetration maps (股权穿透); auditor-client size mismatch as a red flag; pre-archive any evidence that can vanish; always pair fraud claims with a pure valuation attack.
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: Even a correct short draws regulatory and political blowback (SEBI probe into Hindenburg); markets can stay irrational long after the report (Adani shares later rebounded strongly) — publication ≠ immediate payoff.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① Make every allegation independently verifiable so opponents cannot break one link and collapse the chain; ② archive removable evidence before publishing; ③ pair fraud claims with a valuation leg and plan for regulatory retaliation.

### Nikola: How to Short a Truck Company (Hindenburg Research, Nathan Anderson, 2020)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Tested narrative against engineering facts — the "driving" video was the truck rolling downhill; drone footage showed a near-empty prototype facility; "sales" (hydrogen stations) traced to related shells; component provenance checked (Mercedes chassis, unlicensed Bosch fuel cell); ex-employee accounts corroborated.
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: Headline as thesis, then a point-by-point evidence chain; after the company rebutted, follow-up reports rebutted each counter-point (debate-style updating) — the exchange itself became a public audit.
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Annotated video stills, drone imagery, engineering schematics, document scans — visual proof over assertion.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: Physically verify core claims (site visits, video forensics, parts provenance); treat cashless "partnerships/purchase orders" as red flags; keep a rebuttal-response loop to keep the thesis alive in public.
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: Fraud was real (Milton convicted 2022; bankruptcy 2025), yet the trade still depended on an immediate stock drop; retail narrative and momentum can prop a fraudulent stock — timing against narrative momentum is the hard part.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① For "tech miracle" narratives, run engineering fact-checks (video, prototype, capacity) first; ② after a rebuttal, answer each counter-point publicly to keep the thesis alive; ③ fraud proven ≠ immediate decline on hot momentum names — assess momentum before sizing.

### Super Micro: Accounting Manipulation & Self-Dealing (Hindenburg Research, Nathan Anderson, 2024)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Supply-chain audit — flagged supplier companies run by the CEO's brothers as disclosed/suspected-undisclosed related parties used to distort gross margins; cross-checked exports against sanctions lists (Russia); benchmarked gross margins vs Dell/HPE to expose unexplained gaps; used the 2018 filing-delay/restatement history as a Bayesian prior; EY's resignation (unable to rely on management's representations) as confirmation. Three-month investigation.
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: Fraud claims positioned against the "AI momentum winner" market narrative; every allegation sourced; pre-answers the "demand is real so accounting doesn't matter" defense.
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Peer margin-comparison tables, export/customer flow diagrams, accounting-history timelines, Taiwan corporate-registry screenshots.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: Benchmark margins vs peers for unexplained gaps; screen customers/distributors against sanctions lists; treat auditor resignation and 10-K delays as material events; treat restatement history as a risk prior.
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: Accounting red flags do not protect you from momentum — SMCI cratered, then rebounded hard on AI demand; correct thesis + wrong market regime = loss; never short narrative stocks on fundamentals alone; position sizing and exit discipline matter more than the thesis.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① Penetrate related parties down to family-controlled suppliers; ② treat auditor resignation and delayed filings as hard material events; ③ on momentum names, add regime and exit conditions to a fundamental short thesis.

### Luckin Coffee Accounting Fraud (Muddy Waters via Seven Pillars, Carson Block, 2020)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Commissioned field surveillance — 92 full-time + 1,418 part-time staff, 11,260 hours of store video, 981 store-days covering 620 stores across 38 of 53 cities (96% of the store base); measured 263 items/store/day vs the claimed 444–506 (69%/88% inflation in 3Q/4Q 2019); order-log anomalies (one user, 25,843 orders in a day); two-part thesis: fraud + fundamentally broken business.
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: "Smoking-gun" evidence first — independent data wins because management cannot rebut physics; Muddy Waters received the anonymous report and publicly vouched for it, putting its name behind the work.
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Video stills, store maps, per-store volume scatter vs reported figures, order-log screenshots.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: Measure the business physically rather than arguing with the income statement; mirror the company's footprint in sampling design; hunt physically impossible data points as fraud tells.
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: The COO admitted fabricating ~$310M of revenue and the regulatory chain reaction was real (auditor resignation → special committee → SEC fine); yet the stock recovered to pre-fraud levels by 2023 — fraud identified ≠ permanent value destruction; a fellow short (Citron) publicly disagreeing did not make the thesis wrong.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① Cross-validate reported revenue with independent field/monitoring data, sampling to mirror the company's footprint; ② hunt physically impossible data points as smoking guns; ③ after fraud is confirmed, still judge survival/recovery separately — don't default to zero.

### Valeant: Explaining the Pharmacy Relationship (Citron Research, Andrew Left, 2015)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Channel audit — followed revenue into the pharmacy distribution chain; exposed Philidor Rx as a "specialty pharmacy" secretly controlled by Valeant through a call option while publicly labeled independent; price-hike math (acquired drugs up 500–2,000%+); same-store sales maintained by steering patients to the captive pharmacy; questioned booking sales into Philidor as final revenue.
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: Iterative public interrogation — a report titled "Explaining the Pharmacy Relationship" forced disclosure of channel details; each Citron follow-up escalated specificity, making the diligence itself the catalyst.
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Channel-ownership diagrams (who actually controls whom), price-increase tables, same-store sales reconciliations.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: Treat the distribution channel as a black box — trace who really buys the product; check for option/off-balance-sheet control of "independent" partners; treat price-hike sustainability as a red flag.
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: Forcing disclosure can be the trade, but the collapse (~$260→$20) took quarters; Left's own track record is mixed — the same playbook does not always work; channel opacity is where fraud hides.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① Penetrate revenue to the final buyer; suspect "independent" channels controlled via options; ② use escalating public questioning to force disclosure — disclosure itself is the catalyst; ③ know your method's hit rate; short in portfolios with position discipline, not single bets.

### Enron Short Thesis (Kynikos Associates, Jim Chanos, 2001)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Pure public-filing forensics — read the 10-K and found internal inconsistencies: high reported margins/ROE in wholesale trading vs weak operating cash flow; mark-to-market on long-term contracts as a black box; footnote mining surfaced Fastow's LJM/Raptor related-party SPEs and off-balance-sheet leverage. Logic-first: understand the business model; fraud detection was a bonus.
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: Chanos published no report — he built the thesis privately and shorted; journalists (Fortune's Bethany McLean) supplied public validation. You don't need to be the public hero; the thesis can travel through media and time.
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Ratio tables (ROE vs cash flow), footnote extractions, mark-to-market sensitivity logic.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: Internal-consistency checks (earnings vs cash vs margins); footnote mining for related-party SPEs; treat "aggressive but legal" accounting as a thesis, not just fraud hunting; watch accruals-vs-cash divergence.
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: Right and well-timed (short entered 2000, collapse 2001); Chanos's discipline — "the market can stay irrational longer than you can stay solvent" — plus position sizing and knowing when to cover; shorting as a search for truth adds market integrity.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① Run internal-consistency checks first — earnings/cash divergence is the earliest signal; ② related-party SPEs live in the footnotes; mine them; ③ a correct thesis needs no public announcement — let media and time carry it.

### Allied Capital: Fooling Some of the People All of the Time (Greenlight Capital, David Einhorn, 2002)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Attacked the "fair value" accounting of a BDC — private portfolio companies marked up on management's own optimistic assumptions; the company controlled its own scorecard. Einhorn picked a specific portfolio holding and showed the valuation math was fantasy; the SEC later fined Allied over loan-loss accounting (2009).
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: Became a war of narratives — Allied rebutted publicly and the stock initially ROSE against the short; Einhorn turned the thesis into a book ("Fooling Some of the People All of the Time"), compounding one investigation into a long-lived asset.
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Valuation mark-up tables, model-assumptions-vs-reality comparisons.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: When the subject controls the valuation input, demand the assumptions and demonstrate their implausibility; model downside from the company's own marks; convert published research into reputation/career capital.
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: Fighting a company that prints the scorecard is brutal — multi-year, the stock went against him first, requiring staying power and a diversified book; shorting opaque balance sheets is a long, painful grind; being right is necessary but not sufficient.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① Against fair-value accounting, attack the assumptions, not the numbers; ② package research into durable assets (book/presentation) — one investigation, multiple payoffs; ③ before shorting a combative, narrative-strong company, verify capital and nerve for years of adverse moves.

### Wirecard: Exposing a Criminal Enterprise (Financial Times / Zatarra, Dan McCrum, 2019)

- **研究方法 (Methodology)**: Accounting-forensics textbook — ~€1.9B of "cash" sat in Asian trust/escrow accounts that could not be physically verified; asked the simplest questions (meet the trustee? show statements); traced third-party acquiring (TPA) partners processing gambling/adult payments in Asia; a KPMG special audit later found 94% of the Asian escrow statements fabricated; collaborated with short sellers (Zatarra/Fraser Perring) who fed documents to the FT.
- **行文范式 (Argument)**: Journalist-led exposure with named sources plus documents; each FT story was a probe — corporate denials became new evidence; regulator blindness (BaFin's Feb 2019 shorting ban protected the fraud) became part of the story.
- **图表特征 (Evidence Viz)**: Escrow/TPA fund-flow diagrams, trustee org charts, payment-flow maps.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Moves)**: Cash is the easiest asset to fake — verify with a third party directly, never accept trustee-sent screenshots/emails; follow the intermediaries doing the real work; regulator inaction itself is a red flag (politically protected "champions").
- **教训或红旗 (Lessons)**: Germany's largest fraud (€19B lost); EY failed its audit; the short+press model worked but took ~5 years of patience; when cash cannot be verified, everything else is suspect.

**可执行规则 (Executable Rules)**: ① Large cash balances require direct third-party confirmation — screenshots/emails count as possible forgeries; ② fraud hides in third-party intermediaries; trace the agents doing the real work; ③ regulatory silence and political protection are both red flags and the biggest execution obstacles.

## 凝练后这组的核心资产（Transferable Core Assets）

> Eight transferable due-diligence / anti-fraud methods — applicable to the **risk-verification stage of ANY research report**, not just shorts.

1. **Internal-consistency audit (报表内部一致性检验)** — divergence between earnings/ROE and cash flow, or between company margins and peers', is the earliest no-insider signal (Enron/Chanos; SMCI margin benchmarking). Run every "high growth" narrative through this gate first.
2. **Verify cash with a third party, never screenshots (现金物理核验)** — cash is the easiest asset to fake; trust/custody/escrow balances count only when confirmed directly with the counterparty (Wirecard's €1.9B).
3. **Independent measurement beats the income statement (独立数据对抗报表)** — field traffic, satellite, logistics, or store-level data that management cannot rebut (Luckin's 11,260 hours of video; Nikola's drone footage).
4. **Invert the incentive attribution (激励归因倒置)** — study the side paid by volume while holding no risk: lenders not borrowers (Burry), channels not brands (Valeant), suppliers not customers (SMCI).
5. **Ownership & supply-chain penetration maps (股权/供应链穿透制图)** — draw the shell networks, offshore entities, and family-controlled suppliers, then trace the fund flows (Adani's Mauritius shells; SMCI's Liang-brother suppliers).
6. **Auditor-fit red flags + Bayesian priors (审计错配与历史先验)** — a tiny firm auditing a giant conglomerate, auditor resignation, delayed filings, and restatement history all materially raise fraud probability (Adani/Shah Dhandharia; SMCI/EY).
7. **Evidence grading & pre-archiving (证据分级与预归档)** — documentary evidence > corroborated testimony > circumstantial; archive anything that can vanish before publishing (Wayback/Adani auditor site); make every allegation independently verifiable.
8. **Timing & funding mismatch management (时间与资金错配管理)** — "right but too early is functionally wrong" (Burry/Soros): encode conclusions into capped-loss contracts, predefine falsification triggers, confirm the funding structure survives the gap — and always carry a valuation leg independent of the fraud claims.

> **One-line mainline (一句话总纲)**: 做空研究 = 公开信息取证 × 独立验证 × 对抗性论证 × 存活管理 — remove any one of the four and the thesis, however true, will lose money.
