# G3 组凝练 — China & Emerging Markets (10 reports)

## 组总览 (Group Overview)

This group spans very different formats — sell-side strategy notes, a single-stock deep dive, an academic paper, an IMF consultation, and news analysis — but shares one methodological mainline: **(1) the state/policy is a first-class variable**, so trigger-condition checklists and policy-put pricing replace point forecasts; **(2) official aggregates are treated as hypotheses**, and every headline number is cross-checked against self-built micro data, alternative calibers, or foreign-country series; **(3) historical analogies** (Japan's deflation, the 2000 tech bubble, the US adjustment to import shocks) are explicitly translated into "like vs unlike" testable indicator sets; **(4) analysis is split into a fundamental layer (impact on earnings) and a pricing layer (what the market already discounts)**, with action taken only when the two diverge; and **(5) structural slow variables** (demographics, deleveraging, supply chains) are followed through transmission chains to growth, flows, and asset allocation. The group's shared red line: underestimating the persistence of structural forces (regulation, deleveraging, demographic decline) and over-trusting official numbers.

### China Reopening Playbook (Morgan Stanley / Robin Xing & Chetan Ahya, 2022)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Scenario planning instead of point forecasting — three observable preconditions (vaccination coverage, a healthcare-capacity protocol, public tolerance/cognition) are listed as gate conditions and tracked, never timed. Macro scenarios (gradual vs rapid reopening) are then pushed through a transmission chain: sectors (consumers, travel, banks), CNY, and fund flows.
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: Checklist-led exposition — "what to watch" before "what will happen"; every scenario carries a portfolio translation; lessons stated plainly rather than buried in caveats.
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: Vaccination-trajectory scenarios; reopening timeline vs policy events; sector-sensitivity heatmaps of expected alpha per scenario; currency and flows overlays.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① Enumerate observable gate conditions before forecasting; ② attach a portfolio translation to every macro scenario; ③ revisit monthly as conditions confirm or deny.
- **教训/红旗 (Lessons/Red Flags)**: Reopening repaired valuations but not earnings — the playbook target was missed; a scenario framework that ignores earnings delivery fails even when the macro call is right.
- **可执行规则**: Never bet on timing — build a trackable trigger checklist first. Every macro scenario must carry sector/currency/flows transmission. In post-mortems, separate "valuation repair" from "earnings delivery".

### China Equities Strategy: Rebalancing & Policy Support (Goldman Sachs / Kinger Lau, 2023)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Asymmetric-payoff / expectation-gap pricing: first state what prices already imply vs consensus, then itemize the policy put lever by lever (each policy tool gets its own check); cross-market A-share vs H-share comparison; event-driven targets built from three quantified channels — earnings upgrades, risk appetite, fund flows.
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: Top-down strategy note: valuation snapshot vs history → policy timeline → rebalancing winners (mass consumption, TMT) → explicit index targets with the earnings assumption stated (MSCI China +12%, CSI 300 +15% on ~10% EPS growth).
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: Target bridge chart (earnings × valuation × flows); A/H premium time series; policy-event timeline; valuation percentile fans.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① Decompose any target price into explicitly quantified channels; ② itemize policy-put levers and track each; ③ state what consensus prices vs what you price.
- **教训/红旗 (Red Flags)**: Targets hinge on earnings follow-through; policy support without earnings delivery = multiple compression — the dependency must be flagged at the outset.
- **可执行规则**: Break every target into earnings/valuation/flows channels and quantify each. Check policy-put levers item by item. Write "if earnings don't deliver, the target fails" explicitly.

### Evergrande: China Property Deep Dive (UBS / John Lam, 2021)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Cross-caliper verification: reported balance-sheet leverage rebuilt with self-constructed micro data (off-balance-sheet guarantees, JV debt, trust financing) → true net-debt ratio ~170% vs 108% reported. The contradiction between behavior (aggressive land purchases) and numbers (cash claims) triggered the deep dive; cycle reversal anchored to supply clearing (starts/sales scissors), not demand optimism.
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: Contrarian single-stock note, evidence-first; each claim tied to a data construct; anticipates the company rebuttal ("no factual basis") — the report itself became the event.
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: Reconstructed leverage waterfall; cash vs debt-maturity wall; land bank; starts vs sales gap chart.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① Rebuild leverage from off-balance-sheet channels, not just the report; ② verify behavior against numbers and dig on contradiction; ③ use the supply-clearing gap as the reversal anchor.
- **教训/红旗 (Red Flags)**: Being early is painful — the sell call sat 11 months before default while the stock rose; contrarian calls need time tolerance and sizing discipline. (Later reversal: Lam turned bullish in 2024 — same data-driven contrarian method, opposite side.)
- **可执行规则**: Always re-verify reported leverage with self-built micro calibers. Behavior contradicting numbers is a deep-dive trigger. Anchor cycle turns to supply clearing, and price in being early.

### China Tech Crackdown: Look Past, Focus on Fundamentals (HSBC, 2021)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Two-layer regulatory analysis: layer 1 quantifies the policy's impact on the earnings curve and updates targets; layer 2 independently judges whether market pricing has overshot. The buy signal is when the layers conflict — earnings cut but price overcorrected.
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: Framework-first think piece aimed at rattled investors; calm, deliberate tone against panic; explicitly separates "what we know" from "what is priced".
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: Earnings-revision estimates for platform names vs their drawdowns; fundamental-impact vs price-impact comparison.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① For any policy/regulatory shock, quantify the fundamentals layer first; ② price layer second, independently; ③ act only when they diverge.
- **教训/红旗 (Red Flags)**: Underestimating the structural, long-run nature of 2021-22 regulation was the consensus error across foreign investors; treating structural regulation as cyclical produced repeated buy-the-dip losses.
- **可执行规则**: Quantify the earnings impact before judging the price. Buy only when the two layers diverge. Distinguish structural regulation from cyclical disturbance.

### Supply Chains Won't Move Out of China (Yet) (Morgan Stanley, 2020)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Flow vs share dual lens: nominal relocation (announcement flows) vs real decoupling (China's global export share, third-party transshipment, FDI data); Japan historical analogy; direction and speed declared as separate statements.
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: Hypothesis-then-evidence debunking style; calm contrarian published at peak panic (April 2020); explicitly states what would change the view.
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: China's share of global exports time series; relocation-flow counts; cost-comparison tables (China vs Vietnam/India); Japan relocation analogy chart.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① Measure structural shifts in shares, not headlines; ② decompose nominal movement vs real decoupling; ③ declare direction ("will it move?") and speed ("how fast?") separately.
- **教训/红旗 (Red Flags)**: Panic-driven relocation headlines overstated reality — but the thesis is time-bound; the "yet" in the title is the falsification hedge and must be revisited as costs and geopolitics shift.
- **可执行规则**: Judge decoupling by global-share data, not relocation headlines. Separate direction from speed in every claim. The hedge word in your title is your falsification condition — revisit it.

### The China Shock: Labor Market Adjustment (American Economic Review / Autor, Dorn & Hanson, 2013)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Quasi-natural experiment: regional exposure to import competition built as Σ (local industry employment share × national industry import penetration) across ~700-1,400 commuting zones; instrument = China's export growth to other developed markets, exogenous to US local demand; panel regressions with IV.
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: Canonical academic structure: motivating puzzle → data → identification strategy with explicit exogeneity defense → results (employment, wages, labor-force participation, transfers) → robustness checks.
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: Commuting-zone exposure maps; partial-regression plots of outcomes vs instrumented exposure; event-study-style coefficients; import-penetration time series.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① Build share-weighted exposure indices (industry shares × industry shocks); ② instrument endogenous shocks with other-market shocks; ③ check pre-trends; ④ separate aggregate gains from local distributional losses.
- **教训/红旗 (Red Flags)**: Aggregate welfare ≠ distribution — trade's local costs are concentrated and persistent; naive OLS biases toward zero, so identification design is the whole game.
- **可执行规则**: Construct exposure from shares × industry shocks, never raw aggregates. Instrument with other-market shocks and defend exogeneity. Always report the local losers, not just the national winner.

### People's Republic of China: 2023 Article IV Consultation (IMF / IMF Staff, 2023)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Official country-diagnostic template: four blocks — growth rebalancing → property → fiscal → financial risks — each conclusion tied to a data appendix; policy recommendations with sequencing; staff's own scenarios cross-checked against official aggregates.
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: Institutional format: staff appraisal, balanced upside/downside risk assessment, policy advice, consultation record; neutral hedged language; heavy statistical appendix.
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: GDP growth decomposition; property investment/price series; LGFV debt; credit/GDP and debt-service ratios; scenario tables.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① Run the four-block diagnostic as a country checklist; ② pair every conclusion with its data appendix; ③ read alongside national first-hand data to find blind spots.
- **教训/红旗 (Red Flags)**: Multilateral diagnostics understate political-economy constraints and data-quality issues (official stats were revised down post-2018); balanced hedging can obscure priority ordering — read for what is not said.
- **可执行规则**: Cover growth/property/fiscal/financial-risk in every country macro note. Cross-check every official figure against independent data. Treat balanced language as a signal to look for the unsaid.

### China Debt-Deflation Risks: Tracking the Economy (Morgan Stanley / Chetan Ahya & Robin Xing, 2023)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Japan-style balance-sheet recession analogy made testable: enumerate "like" indicators (credit impulse, private-sector deleveraging, property prices, PPI/wage dynamics) and "unlike" indicators (capital controls, state-sector share, policy toolkit), then track the series month by month through the deflation spiral.
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: Analogy-driven narrative with an explicit indicator checklist; policy-response analysis; clear tracking cadence ("this is what we watch every month").
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: Japan vs China overlay charts (credit impulse, property prices, PPI); debt-service ratios; policy response timeline.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① For any historical analogy, write the like/unlike indicator list; ② anchor deflation risk to credit impulse and private-sector balance sheets; ③ state which policy change would break the spiral.
- **教训/红旗 (Red Flags)**: Analogies are maps, not destinations — China's state capacity and capital controls differ materially; watch the "unlike" conditions themselves (e.g., weak policy transmission) rather than assuming they hold.
- **可执行规则**: Translate every analogy into like-vs-unlike indicators and track them. Anchor deflation to credit impulse, not CPI headlines. Spell out the policy condition that would falsify the spiral.

### India Stocks: The Bull Market's Next Phase (Morgan Stanley / Ridham Desai, 2024)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Bull-market stage theory: policy cycle → foreign flows → earnings-upgrade transmission chain; elections and reforms translated into catalysts and risks, not narratives; market-structure analysis (domestic SIP flows as a stabilizing marginal buyer).
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: Thematic strategy note: phase framework → current position in the cycle → catalysts → risks; accessible narrative for broad investors with a clear "what changes my view" section.
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: Bull-market phase diagram; FII vs DII flow series; earnings-growth trajectory vs valuation; reform timeline.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① Locate the market in a stage framework before forecasting; ② translate political events into earnings/flows catalysts; ③ analyze the marginal buyer (domestic vs foreign) for sustainability.
- **教训/红旗 (Red Flags)**: Phase frameworks self-confirm until the transmission breaks — elections are catalysts, not fundamentals; verify that earnings actually follow flows before extending the thesis.
- **可执行规则**: Place the market in a stage framework first. Convert events into earnings and flows catalysts. Track the marginal buyer's structure before trusting any trend.

### China's Population Shrinks for the First Time in 60+ Years (NBS data via Al Jazeera, 2023)

- **研究方法 (Research Method)**: Data fact-check of an official release: births, deaths, and working-age figures cross-checked against series context; distinguishes one-off factors (COVID-year deaths, delayed marriages) from structural trend; treats demography as a slow variable and follows transmission chains rather than trading the headline.
- **行文范式 (Writing Paradigm)**: News-analysis hybrid: official numbers first, then interpretation with explicit caveats; quotes officials and demographers; refuses single-datum panic.
- **图表特征 (Chart Style)**: Population and birth-rate time series; age pyramid; birth-death crossover chart.
- **可复用标准动作 (Reusable Actions)**: ① Ask of any data point: blip or turn? (check series context); ② separate cyclical from structural drivers; ③ trace slow variables through growth → consumption → asset-allocation chains.
- **教训/红旗 (Red Flags)**: One data point is not a trend (births rebounded briefly in following years); Chinese official statistics carry reliability caveats; demographic impacts are decades-long — they inform allocation, not timing.
- **可执行规则**: Classify every data point as blip or trend before interpreting. Separate cyclical from structural drivers. Use slow variables for transmission-chain reasoning, never for timing.

## 凝练后这组的核心资产 (Core Assets of This Group)

1. **Trigger-condition checklist** — enumerate observable gate conditions (vaccination, policy levers, capacity) before any forecast; track them and never bet on timing (Reopening Playbook, Kinger Lau).
2. **Cross-caliper verification** — rebuild every official/company number with independent micro data and compare; a contradiction is a research trigger, not an anomaly (UBS 170% vs 108%; population release vs series).
3. **Testable historical analogy** — turn "it's like Japan / 2000 / the US import shock" into like-vs-unlike indicator lists tracked monthly (Debt-Deflation, Supply Chains).
4. **Two-layer policy analysis** — quantify the earnings impact first, then judge pricing independently; act only when the layers diverge (HSBC).
5. **Exposure-index + instrument design** — share-weighted regional exposure with other-market shocks as an IV: a causal-identification template for any shock study (China Shock).
6. **Flow vs share decomposition** — separate nominal movement from real structural change; declare direction and speed as separate statements (Supply Chains).
7. **Three-channel target bridge** — decompose every target price into earnings + risk appetite + fund flows, each quantified, with the failure dependency stated (Kinger Lau).
8. **Slow-variable transmission mapping** — demography and leverage followed through growth → consumption → asset allocation, never used for timing (Population, Debt-Deflation).
