# G10 Digest: Thematic, Alternative & Event Research（主题 / 另类 / 事件研究）

## Group Overview（组总览）

The methodological spine of G10 is a single rule: **a narrative becomes an investment thesis only after it is anchored to falsifiable, physically measurable, or official data.** Three method families recur. (1) **Thematic frameworks** (MS 2030 Outlook, GS 6 Ps): start from a "world-scale necessity" proposition (labor, power, geopolitics), then force every theme to carry a quantity anchor, a date, a cross-industry winner/loser transmission chain, and a written counter-case; TAM becomes credible only when attached to a bottleneck checklist and bottom-up aggregation. (2) **Event/mechanism studies** (GameStop, negative oil, Terra-LUNA, chip shortage): decompose extreme outcomes into participant rules × feedback loops × institutional/physical constraints, replacing sentiment narratives, and always ask who is the forced transactor. (3) **Narrative datafication and anti-bubble discipline** (BLS "Great Resignation", Grantham, Taleb): rebuild media concepts from primary official series and stress-test "this time is different" against long-history percentiles and fat tails. Recurring red flags across the group: TAM without bottlenecks, mechanism "guarantees" untested under incentive reversal, and identification claims silently converted into timing calls.

### Tech and Innovation: 2030 Outlook (Morgan Stanley, Ed Stanley, 2024)（主题框架·顶部-底部）
- **研究方法 Method**: Top-down thematic investing: starts from world-scale "necessity" propositions (labor scarcity, power, geopolitics, data) rather than sector popularity; maps each theme into cross-industry winner/loser transmission chains; every theme carries falsifiable quantity anchors and a timetable so the narrative can be tested as it unfolds.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Proposition → evidence → transmission chain → beneficiaries/exposed → explicit bear case; counterarguments are written inside each theme, not buried in a risk section.
- **图表特征 Charts**: Multi-horizon adoption S-curves marked with current position; winner/loser chain diagrams spanning industries; quantity milestones plotted against a timeline for later verification.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: Define the binding necessity (labor/power) first; anchor each theme in a measurable quantity (GWh/units); attach a date; write the counter-thesis in the same document.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: Theme portfolios decay into pure narrative when the anchor number is missing or the timeline is open-ended; "world-scale necessity" alone is not a tradeable thesis.
**可执行规则**: Every theme must carry one falsifiable quantity plus a date; write the counter-thesis inside the same document; require a transmission chain (who wins/loses and via what mechanism) before calling it a trade.

### Data Center Power Demand: The 6 Ps (Goldman Sachs GS SUSTAIN, 2024)（TAM 接瓶颈）
- **研究方法 Method**: Dual-method cross-validation: top-down power budget (GW from AI capex scenarios) × bottom-up project-level aggregation; explicitly models the binding constraints via a 6-P bottleneck checklist (power, permits, poles & wires, pipes/gas, panels, people) instead of reporting only total demand.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Scale anchor → constraint enumeration → scenario band → implications per supply chain; the "equivalent country" analogy calibrates magnitude (data centers ≈ a mid-size nation's electricity use).
- **图表特征 Charts**: Demand stacked bars by driver with scenario ranges; constraint heatmaps; build-out lead-time sensitivities; each constraint's capacity ceiling vs demand.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: Always cross-check top-down TAM against bottom-up counts; rank constraints by which binds first; express demand in physical units (GW) with an analogy a reader can feel.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: Total-demand-only forecasts (classic TAM abuse) look right in aggregate but break at the bottleneck; lead times (permits, grid connections) decide who actually wins, not the demand slope.
**可执行规则**: Never publish a demand number without naming the bottleneck that binds first; cross-validate top-down and bottom-up within stated bands; anchor magnitudes with a physical analogy.

### Waiting for the Last Dance (GMO, Jeremy Grantham, 2021)（泡沫识别清单）
- **研究方法 Method**: Bubble identification as a four-element checklist — extreme valuations (long-history percentiles), accelerating price action, irrationality markers (retail leverage, record issuance), systemic fragility — stacking independent evidence types rather than relying on a single signal.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Historical-analogy narrative (past bubbles) with explicit valuation metrics per era; sober framing that this is "a prediction of damage, not of timing."
- **图表特征 Charts**: Century-long valuation percentile charts; peak-by-peak comparisons; drawdown scenarios showing asymmetric downside even if one exits late.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: Build a multi-decade valuation percentile database in advance; pre-write the bubble criteria; separate the identification claim (expensive?) from the timing claim (when does it burst?).
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: The classic value-investor error is converting an identification call into a market-timing call; being early and loud destroys credibility even when ultimately right.
**可执行规则**: Keep the identification claim and the timing claim as two separate statements; score bubbles against a pre-written checklist; express downside scenarios with probabilities, not dates.

### The GameStop Short Squeeze (Wikipedia / Reuters, 2021)（微观结构事件研究）
- **研究方法 Method**: Market-microstructure cascade event study: retail order flow → options gamma (dealers hedging deltas) → short covering → retail FOMO; maps the positive feedback loop and shows how new structure (zero-commission apps, social-media coordination) changed shorting cost and risk calculus.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Chronological blow-by-blow with mechanism interleaved; timeline table of key days and prices; attribution to structure rather than "irrational retail."
- **图表特征 Charts**: Price/volume timeline with short-interest and options-OI overlays; gamma-squeeze schematic; borrow-rate and short-interest-ratio panels.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: For any squeeze/run event, list the feedback links, identify which actor's hedging rules amplify (dealers, ETFs), and check structural preconditions (borrow cost, options volume) before attributing to sentiment.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: "Retail irrationality" is a lazy explanation; the same dynamics are identifiable beforehand from borrow rates and gamma exposure; post-hoc regulatory debate often misses the mechanism.
**可执行规则**: Model squeezes as actor-rule feedback loops, not sentiment; check borrow cost plus dealer gamma before calling anything a squeeze; attribute events to structure before narrative.

### WTI Oil Futures Turn Negative (U.S. Congressional Research Service, 2020)（制度机制拆解）
- **研究方法 Method**: Institutional/mechanism event study: negative prices as the product of physical storage saturation + futures delivery mechanics + ETF rollover forced selling, decomposed step by step; treats extreme price as a constraint artifact, not a supply-demand signal.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Regulatory explainer: question → timeline of the May contract → settlement mechanics → who got hurt and why; plain language with the legal/mechanical chain in order.
- **图表特征 Charts**: Storage utilization vs futures-curve inversion; May–June contract price split; margin/rollover loss illustration for retail ETF holders.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: When a price breaks "impossible" levels, enumerate the physical and institutional constraints (storage, delivery, expiry, roll schedules) before any narrative; identify who is forced to transact and when.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: Headline WTI diverged wildly from what most participants actually traded (far months, Brent, retail ETFs); retail products can amplify institutional mechanics.
**可执行规则**: Explain extreme prints via the constraint chain, not a demand story; identify forced transactors and expiry mechanics; distinguish the headline index from the instrument your reader actually holds.

### The Black Swan: The Impact of the Highly Improbable (NYU / book, Nassim Taleb, 2007)（肥尾与反脆弱）
- **研究方法 Method**: A philosophical-statistical critique turned into an operating framework: Gaussian extrapolation fails on fat tails; the response is not better prediction but robustness — position sizing, exposure caps, optionality ("antifragility") — so that unknown shocks are survivable or even beneficial.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Provocative essay: narrative episodes (9/11, dot-com, LTCM) plus epistemic argument; defines reusable vocabulary (Black Swan, Mediocristan/Extremistan).
- **图表特征 Charts**: Deliberately sparse: simple distribution sketches contrasting thin vs fat tails; evidence is mostly historical anecdote used as logical proof, not data series.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: Stress-test portfolios against shocks that "cannot happen"; build a barbell (safe core + speculative optionality); treat historical VaR as a floor of ignorance, not a risk measure.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: The framework itself flags backtest complacency — rare events are by definition underrepresented in samples; it explains after the fact far better than it predicts.
**可执行规则**: Size positions so the worst plausible surprise is survivable; treat model confidence as weakest exactly where history is calm; use barbell/convexity to profit from uncertainty.

### Anatomy of a Run: The Terra-LUNA Crash (MIT Sloan CFI, 2022)（机制设计压力测试）
- **研究方法 Method**: Case-study decomposition of an algorithmic-stablecoin death spiral: documents the arbitrage loop (LUNA mint ↔ UST peg), traces how a depeg converts the "guarantee" into a self-reinforcing sell engine, and shows consensus collapse as the terminal state; tests the mechanism-design claim under incentive reversal.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Academic case structure: mechanism schematic → run timeline → who acted and when → lessons; measured tone, attributing to design rather than a fraud narrative.
- **图表特征 Charts**: Peg-deviation vs supply charts; death-spiral flow schematic; timeline of key whale transactions and protocol actions.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: For any "algorithmic guarantee" (stablecoins, basis trades, arbitrage mechanisms), model each actor's incentive under both normal and stressed states; check whether the mechanism's own participants become forced sellers when it breaks.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: Mechanism-design guarantees hold only where incentives do not reverse; unbacked claims (TVL, peg promises) are narrative until a real withdrawal test.
**可执行规则**: Stress-test every algorithmic guarantee by inverting incentives; ask who is forced to sell in the failure state; treat unbacked claims (peg, yield) as narrative until a real withdrawal test.

### Chip Shortage: $110B Auto Industry Cost (CNBC, 2021)（冲击传导量化）
- **研究方法 Method**: Supply-chain shock quantification through the propagation chain: one bottleneck (auto-grade chips) amplified via JIT scheduling discipline, dealer inventory, and production lines into a headline $110B revenue loss; shows how a localized shortage becomes an industry-wide loss.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Journalism-to-research hybrid: one quantified headline, then mechanism, then losers/winners, then duration; concrete dollar anchors throughout.
- **图表特征 Charts**: Revenue-loss waterfall by OEM; inventory days vs production cuts; chip lead-time and price trends; bottleneck-to-loss propagation diagram.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: For any supply shock, identify the single binding input, quantify its share in the product, then trace amplification (production stops, lost revenue) rather than citing the shortage itself as the cost; check inventory buffers at each node.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: Aggregate "shortage" headlines hide that losses concentrate where JIT buffers are thinnest; the $110B figure depends on modeling assumptions — trace the number to its waterfall.
**可执行规则**: Trace the bottleneck → production → revenue waterfall instead of quoting headline cost; check JIT/buffer inventory per node; attribute losses to buffer policy, not just scarcity.

### The 'Great Resignation' in Perspective (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2022)（叙事数据化）
- **研究方法 Method**: Narrative datafication: rebuilds a media-coined concept from official series (quits rate, labor-force participation, vacancies), decomposing it into structural vs cyclical vs transient components with dates and magnitude.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Data-corrective essay: state the popular claim → show the official series → disaggregate → verdict (mostly cyclical/transient in parts) → implications; lets numbers arbitrate a media story.
- **图表特征 Charts**: Seasonally adjusted quits rates over decades; participation rates by age/sex; quits vs vacancies overlays; all with source callouts — no invented series.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: When a media narrative dominates, go to the primary statistical agency first; decompose the aggregate by group and time; classify structural vs cyclical vs transient before making claims.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: The narrative inflated a modest cyclical phenomenon into an epochal shift; official-data-first is the cheapest antidote to narrative drift; later data revisions can rewrite the story.
**可执行规则**: Rebuild every hot concept from primary official data; decompose by demographic and time before concluding; classify structural vs cyclical before acting.

### Operation Warp Speed: Accelerated Vaccine Development (U.S. Government via Wikipedia, 2020)（制度设计加速）
- **研究方法 Method**: Institutional-design case study of extreme acceleration: parallel (not sequential) development, advance-purchase agreements to shift risk, rolling regulatory review — quantifying how institutional choices compressed a decade-long pipeline into months.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Government retrospective: mission framing → problem → design choices → execution timeline → dated outcomes; systematic about the institutional levers used.
- **图表特征 Charts**: Timeline-compression diagram (normal vs OWS development paths); Gantt-style parallel tracks; funding commitments by company.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: For any "impossible timeline," identify which sequential gates can run in parallel, who bears which risk (pre-purchase contracts), and which regulatory constraints can be relaxed with oversight; apply the same lens to capex and tech-adoption timelines.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: The success is not replicable without a government risk backstop; celebrating outcomes without the risk-sharing structure misleads; single-vendor dependence and compressed trials carry tail risks.
**可执行规则**: Compress timelines by parallelizing gates and pre-committing demand, not by hoping; name who bears each risk; treat accelerated outcomes as conditional on that risk structure.

## 凝练后这组的核心资产（Core Transferable Assets）

1. **Necessity ladder + falsifiable anchor**（MS 2030）: every thematic claim must carry one falsifiable quantity and a date, plus a written counter-thesis; no anchor = narrative, not a thesis.
2. **TAM must be attached to a bottleneck checklist**（GS 6 Ps）: a demand number has investment meaning only alongside "which constraint binds first and how long is its lead time"; total-demand-only is classic TAM abuse.
3. **Top-down × bottom-up dual cross-validation**: budget math and project-level aggregation must agree within stated bands; the divergence itself is the finding (a gap = bottleneck or overestimated demand).
4. **Physical analogy to anchor magnitude**（equivalent country / storage tanks / quits rate）: calibrate numbers with scales a reader can feel, in physical units (GW, barrels, official series) — never "market potential."
5. **Events = participant rules × feedback loops × institutional constraints**（GameStop / negative oil / LUNA）: before any narrative, walk the mechanism chain — who is the forced transactor, whose hedging rule amplifies, which institutional link failed.
6. **Mechanism "guarantees" require incentive-reversal testing**（Terra-LUNA）: model the stressed state where the mechanism's own participants become forced sellers; unbacked claims (peg, TVL, yield) are narrative until a real withdrawal/depeg test.
7. **Narrative datafication in three questions**（BLS）: rebuild the media concept from primary official series; split by group and time; classify structural vs cyclical vs transient before acting.
8. **Identification ≠ timing**（Grantham / Taleb）: write "is it expensive" and "when does it burst" as two separate statements; stack long-history percentiles plus behavioral evidence; under fat tails, use barbell structure and exposure caps rather than prediction accuracy.
