# G6 Group Digest — Consumer & Healthcare / Industry Themes (10 reports)

> 凝练子代理 G6 组输出。目的：把这 10 篇消费/医疗行业研报的方法论骨架拆出来，供 wall-street-research-methodology skill 吸收。Each entry focuses on *how* the report argues, not what it concludes.

## Group Overview — 方法论主线

G6 组（消费/医疗行业主题）共用一条主线：**自下而上的需求测量**。与宏观组从总量出发不同，这组研报一律先把"消费者/患者"拆成可数的单位——人口基数、渗透率、客单价×客单量、购物篮、客户分层、城市线级——再在这些单位上建立论点。第二条共用主线是**降级/求值(trading-down)的多角度三角验证**：同一现象(消费者转向价值)分别用价格-销量背离(Bain/Reuters)、自有品牌份额(McKinsey)、客单价与折扣依赖(UBS)、热量消费模型(BofA/GLP-1)独立测量，互证后才下结论。第三条主线是**调查信号必须与硬数据互证**：AlphaWise 面板、UBS Evidence Lab、BoF-McKinsey 消费者调查都强调陈述性偏好(stated preference)≠真实行为(revealed behavior)，样本量/抽样框/误差范围被显式披露。第四条是**跨域翻译**：临床终点(vaccine efficacy)、政策条文(CBO 谈判定价)、疗法机制(GLP-1)都要被翻译成市场规模、定价与跨行业传导链条。第五条是**事件驱动重估**：单一数据事件(三期中期分析、CEO 一句评论、政策打分)如何重排整个板块估值——以及披露个案数、样本量与时间窗的证据纪律。

### Anti-Obesity Drug Market: A $100B+ Opportunity (Goldman Sachs / Chris Shibutani, 2023)

- **研究方法 Method**: TAM multiplier chain — epidemiology base (obese/overweight adult population) × penetration scenario (~13% of eligible US adults ≈ 15M treated by 2030) × payer/reimbursement rate × price × duration of use. The institutional inflection (AMA 2013 declaring obesity a chronic disease) anchors the TAM; market history (old drugs 3–11% weight loss) frames the new drug class as the unlock. Explicit sensitivity disclosure: author states the $100B number is "relatively sensitive to minor tweaks" to underlying drivers.
- **行文范式 Writing**: one headline number → bottom-up driver decomposition → efficacy step-change narrative (early gen vs incretin mid-20%) → cost-burden context (CDC $173B obesity costs) → analyst caveat on own model. The caveat is part of the argument, not an afterthought.
- **图表特征 Charts**: market ramp trajectory from $6B run-rate to $100B by 2030; penetration ramp; efficacy comparison bars across drug generations; obesity cost-burden bars.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) build TAM as explicit multiplier product, never a single headline; (2) show which multiplier each sensitivity hits; (3) anchor the TAM to a regime/institutional change (chronic-disease designation) that persists.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: single-point headline ($100B) is the risk even when the model is honest — readers keep the number, drop the ranges; penetration rests on reimbursement assumptions that proved volatile (GLP-1 coverage battles).
- **可执行规则**: TAM 永远写成"基数×渗透率×支付方×价格×疗程"的乘数链，每个乘数做敏感性披露；用制度性拐点(慢性病定义)锚定市场规模，而非纯需求外推；自己先说出"这个数字最脆弱在哪"。

### AlphaWise: The Convenience Premium (Morgan Stanley / AlphaWise, 2024)

- **研究方法 Method**: proprietary recurring consumer panel (~2,000 respondents, monthly rolling waves, cross-year comparable — the Consumer Pulse Survey is in its 47th wave); survey asks both stated-intent and behavior questions; signals are cross-validated against official macro data and handed to sector analysts (hospitality, staples, internet, media) for industry-level mapping. Core finding used as illustration: 77% of US consumers cite convenience (comfort/speed/accessibility/availability) as a key purchase factor.
- **行文范式 Writing**: "key takeaways" upfront, one consumer trait → list of beneficiary sectors; every claim tied to a survey wave date.
- **图表特征 Charts**: stacked bars of purchase-decision drivers; cohort splits (income/age); time series showing the convenience premium vs price sensitivity across waves.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) build a fixed rolling panel so trends are comparable across waves/years; (2) translate one survey trait into a concrete sector map; (3) treat survey as leading signal and always pair with hard data.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: stated preference ≠ revealed behavior — consumers say "convenience" but buy on price; the panel self-reports need triangulation with actual spend/basket data; a single cross-section cannot separate trait from trend.
- **可执行规则**: 调查要建固定滚动面板(月度波次、跨年可比)才谈趋势；任何调查结论必须挂波次日期与样本量；陈述性偏好只能当先行假设，必须用真实消费数据互证。

### The State of Fashion 2024 (McKinsey x The Business of Fashion, 2024)

- **研究方法 Method**: annual industry outlook layered from macro/geopolitical scenarios → McKinsey Global Fashion Index (economic profit segmented by price tier: luxury / premium-bridge / mid-market / value-discount) → proprietary executive survey → consumer survey (US/UK/France/China, ~1,959 respondents in the 2025 edition fielded Aug–Oct) → 10 named themes per edition. "Resilience / slowdown" claims are anchored to the *same metric measured across consecutive years* — no metric, no claim.
- **行文范式 Writing**: theme-by-theme chapters; each theme = exec-survey stat + consumer-survey stat + data exhibit + strategic implication box; a glossary of definitions (AOV, EP) is included so readers share the same口径.
- **图表特征 Charts**: MGFI economic-profit by segment; survey bar charts; region heatmaps; "Super Winners" (Inditex/Fast Retailing/TJX) continuous-EP charts.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) make every qualitative word ("resilient", "trading down") operational with a repeated indicator across years; (2) segment before aggregating — mid-market and luxury move opposite ways; (3) pair executive and consumer surveys so claims rest on two independent respondent groups.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: survey-based claims are self-selected and perishable — each edition is a version, not the truth; cross-year comparability breaks if survey design changes.
- **可执行规则**: "韧性/放缓"这类定性词必须绑定同一指标连续年份的对比；先分价格带/客群再下行业结论；高管调查与消费者调查双源互证，缺一不下趋势判断。

### Luxury Study: Luxury in Transition (Bain & Company / Altagamma, 2024)

- **研究方法 Method**: 23rd edition of an annual market study; total market built bottom-up across 9 segments (cars, personal luxury goods, hospitality = 80% of €1.48T); demand-tier decomposition (top clients vs aspirational/entry Gen Z) used to judge *quality* of growth; price-vs-volume divergence used as turning-point signal (price hikes continued while volumes fell → first personal-luxury contraction in 15 years); customer-base counting (shrank ~50M in two years); brand-performance polarization (only ~1/3 of brands grew in 2024 vs 95% in 2021–22); constant vs current exchange rates reported separately.
- **行文范式 Writing**: exec summary with headline numbers → segment trends → regional breakdown (Japan up, mainland China down) → distribution channels (outlets outperforming full-price) → category detail → forward outlook with explicit uncertainty ("highly dependent on macro scenarios").
- **图表特征 Charts**: market size by segment; regional growth bars; customer-tier share decomposition; price vs volume decomposition; brand polarization chart; outlet vs full-price channel split.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) decompose growth into price effect × volume effect × customer count; (2) watch tier divergence — top clients still buying while the base shrinks = an offer/quality problem, not a demand problem; (3) use polarization of brand performance (share of brands growing) as the aggregate health metric.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: figures are estimates with ranges (±1%), not measurements; customer-base counting methodology is not fully transparent; current vs constant exchange rates can flip the headline — always check both.
- **可执行规则**: 增长必须拆成价格效应×销量效应×客户数三层，价格销量背离是行业拐点先行信号；客户分层(核心客vs入门客)决定增长"质量"；报市场数据时同时给当前汇率与恒定汇率口径。

### GLP-1 Drugs Raise Alarms About Food Demand (Analyst notes via Markets Insider, 2023)

- **研究方法 Method**: cross-industry transmission study — one therapy (weight-loss drugs) repriced along the "pharma → food/beverage/restaurant" chain; hypothesis validated in layers: company-management data (Walmart CEO: slight basket pullback, "less units, slightly less calories" from anonymized pharmacy-linked spending) → patient survey (Morgan Stanley, n=300: 77% visit fast food less; calorie intake −20–30%) → aggregate consumption model (BofA US calorie model: −1–3% total, risk ranking alcohol > snacks > beverages).
- **行文范式 Writing**: event-driven news synthesis — stock-price action as first evidence, then mechanism (GLP-1 satiety), then quantification layers from weakest to strongest; each layer attributed to its source.
- **图表特征 Charts**: stock price move table (PEP/KO −6%, MCD −5%, WMT/COST −4%); patient survey bars; calorie-consumption model output.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) when a shock crosses industries, chain evidence node-by-node from source data (company disclosure, basket data) → consumer behavior → aggregate model; (2) rank category exposure by substitution risk rather than just revenue overlap; (3) label the evidence layer of every number.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: a single CEO comment moved 5–6% of staple stocks while underlying data was still thin (9M patients; n=300 survey) — event-driven pricing detached from fundamentals; early-stage extrapolation is the classic overreach.
- **可执行规则**: 跨行业冲击要沿链条逐节点找证据(管理层数据→行为调查→总量模型)，每层标注来源与样本量；按替代风险给暴露行业排序，而非按营收重叠；警惕单一事件驱动的板块级价格波动脱离基本面。

### Medicare Drug Price Negotiation (Congressional Budget Office, 2023)

- **研究方法 Method**: policy cost-estimation paradigm — translate the statute ("negotiation") into parameterized price-discount ranges (CBO negotiation model: 57–75% price reduction vs current prices depending on data/parameters) → budget impact over the 10-year scoring window (~$100B federal savings 2026–31 on first 10 drugs; $25B deficit reduction in 2031); explicitly separates short-run price savings from long-run innovation-incentive (dynamic) effects; assumptions stated so anyone can re-run the model.
- **行文范式 Writing**: institutional scorekeeping — mechanism description, parameter sensitivity, ranges instead of false precision, no advocacy; conclusion follows the scoring window definition.
- **图表特征 Charts**: budget-impact tables by provision; price-reduction scenarios across parameter values; drug-by-drug savings breakdown.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) policy analysis = statute → mechanism → parameterized price/budget effect → scored output, with every assumption labeled; (2) always split static (price) from dynamic (innovation/R&D) effects; (3) state which conclusion depends on which assumption.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: wide estimate ranges — the number is only as good as the parameters; dynamic R&D effects are the most contested and least verifiable; scores get revised as the law's implementation details emerge — treat any institutional score as versioned.
- **可执行规则**: 政策测算四步走：法条→机制→参数化价格效应→预算打分，假设全部显式标注；静态价格效应与动态创新激励必须分开陈述；记住任何机构打分的结论都依赖评分窗口与参数，事后要重新打分。

### Eyes on China's Consumer Behavior (UBS Evidence Lab, 2024)

- **研究方法 Method**: survey-based consumer research with census-matched sampling — China Brand Equity Survey: n=1,800, online, fieldwork 4 Mar–15 Apr 2024, distribution mirrored by census age/gender/city-tier, MOE ±1.84% @90% CI; Low-tier Cities survey: 5th wave, n=2,000, Tier 1–3 cities, category-purchase decision-maker qualification. Trading-down is measured through *unit economics* — average ticket, discount/promotion dependence, channel-structure shifts — not total retail sales; store checks and official data used for cross-validation.
- **行文范式 Writing**: Evidence Lab format — methodology box (sample, fieldwork window, MOE) stated before any finding; findings grouped by city tier and category.
- **图表特征 Charts**: tier-city survey bars; trade-down heatmaps by category; channel-mix shift charts; discount-dependence splits.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) publish sampling frame, fieldwork window, and margin of error with every survey claim; (2) detect trade-down via ticket size / discount rate / channel mix, not top-line growth; (3) segment by city tier — Tier-1 and low-tier consumers diverge in both direction and mechanism.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: online surveys over-represent digital-savvy consumers; stated confidence ≠ actual spending; a single wave cannot separate noise from trend — hence the 5th-wave series design.
- **可执行规则**: 每个调查结论必须带抽样框、调研窗口与误差范围；降级识别看客单价/折扣率/渠道结构而非社零总量；城市线级必须分层，一线与低线行为机制不同。

### Pfizer & BioNTech mRNA Vaccine Efficacy (Pfizer / BioNTech, 2020)

- **研究方法 Method**: event-evidence reporting of a Phase 3 interim analysis — case-count discipline: 94 confirmed COVID cases evaluated, 43,538 enrolled (42% diverse backgrounds), efficacy >90% at 7 days post-second-dose (=28 days from start), independent DMC review, no serious safety signals, final analysis pre-committed at 164 cases, EUA submission planned after the required safety milestone. Every claim carries its statistical basis and time window; interim vs final explicitly distinguished.
- **行文范式 Writing**: bullet-led press release — headline efficacy number → study design → next steps with dates; interim caveat ("final efficacy percentage may vary") stated in the body.
- **图表特征 Charts**: minimal — the "chart" is the efficacy-by-case-count table and the case-split between vaccine and placebo arms; the market's reaction charts (biotech re-rating, staples de-rating) live in the follow-on research, not the press release.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) for clinical/biotech events, translate endpoints (efficacy %, safety profile) into market size, pricing, and adoption scenarios; (2) always state interim vs final data and sample/case counts — they set the confidence interval; (3) map which adjacent sectors re-price when the endpoint lands.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: >90% on 94 cases carries wide confidence intervals — a press-release number is not the label; one event repriced an entire sector, so event-driven repricing risk must be modeled explicitly.
- **可执行规则**: 医药事件研究把临床终点翻译成市场规模/定价/渗透三件套；区分中期与最终数据、写明病例数与时间窗，它们决定置信度；单一临床数据发布能重排整个板块估值，重估风险要显式建模。

### A Turning Point for Private Label Brands (McKinsey, 2021)

- **研究方法 Method**: private-label share used as the quantified indicator of consumer trading down; consumer-survey evidence (80%+ of US consumers rate private-brand food quality same-or-better than national brands; ~90% see similar-or-better value; 1 in 3 switched to more private brands during the pandemic); retailer-side analysis of gross-margin structure, price-band competition, SKU-level cost management; capability framework (merchandising/brand building, insights-led product development, next-generation sourcing; private-brand COE as the operating model).
- **行文范式 Writing**: consulting insight pattern — "turning point" framing, survey evidence → strategic implication for each stakeholder (retailers vs CPG brands); numbered exhibits.
- **图表特征 Charts**: consumer perception bars; private-label penetration vs national-brand trend lines; retailer margin-structure exhibits.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) use private-label share/penetration as the tradable, trackable metric for trading down; (2) analyze both demand side (consumer perception) and supply side (retailer margin & capability) — a turning point needs both to confirm; (3) when claiming a structural shift, back it with a survey *and* market-share data, not either alone.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: perception surveys ("rate quality same or better") carry known self-report bias; US vs European market dynamics differ; pandemic-era behavior may not persist — structural claims need post-period confirmation.
- **可执行规则**: 用自有品牌份额作消费降级的可跟踪量化指标；结构性拐点必须需求侧(消费者感知)+供给侧(零售商毛利/能力)双确认；感知类调查结论要留出自我报告偏差并等疫后数据验证。

### McDonald's Value Menu War: Muted Fast-Food Traffic (Reuters, 2024)

- **研究方法 Method**: same-store sales decomposition into traffic (客单量) × average check (客单价) — discounting is dragging check while traffic stays negative = structural demand weakness, not inflation-driven growth; triangulated with third-party foot-traffic data (Placer.ai: MCD visits +2.4%, Taco Bell +3.8%, Chipotle +10% in Jan–Mar), broker commentary (BTIG: traffic negative, check under pressure), price-target trims (≥5 brokerages in April), and income-cohort segmentation (low-income consumers hesitant to eat out).
- **行文范式 Writing**: news template with labeled blocks — lede → "the context" → "the fundamentals" (same-store expectations: MCD +2.36% global; EPS $2.72) → "Wall Street sentiment" (ratings, median targets from LSEG); each block separates fact from expectation.
- **图表特征 Charts**: same-store-sales trend line; traffic vs check decomposition; stock performance table (MCD −8% YTD vs Chipotle +40%); foot-traffic bars by chain.
- **可复用标准动作 Reusable moves**: (1) always decompose same-store sales into traffic × ticket — discount-driven ticket cuts with negative traffic means demand weakness, not a healthy price war; (2) use third-party foot traffic as a leading cross-check before earnings; (3) separate company-specific vs segment-wide vs macro factors before attributing cause.
- **教训或红旗 Lessons**: analysts extrapolate quarterly trends into full-year calls; value-menu wars are zero-sum share grabs that can mask category stagnation; Q1 weather is a confounder.
- **可执行规则**: 同店销售一律拆成客单量×客单价，折扣压价+客流为负=需求弱而非促销有效；用第三方客流数据做财报前的先行交叉验证；归因前先分清公司特有/行业/宏观三层因素。

## 凝练后这组的核心资产 (Transferable Core Assets)

1. **TAM 乘数链 (TAM multiplier chain)** — 市场=人口基数×渗透率×支付方×价格×疗程/用量，每个乘数单独做敏感性披露(高盛减重药/GLP-1)。宁给区间不给假精确单点。
2. **需求分解三件套 (Demand decomposition)** — 增长拆成价格效应×销量效应×客户数/客单量×客单价；价格与销量(或客流量)背离是最早的拐点信号(Bain、Reuters)。
3. **降级检测工具箱 (Trade-down detection toolkit)** — 自有品牌份额、客单价、折扣依赖度、渠道结构、outlet 占比、热量消费模型：同一现象用多个独立指标测量并互证(McKinsey、UBS、BofA)。
4. **调查纪律 (Survey discipline)** — 固定滚动面板、普查匹配抽样、显式披露样本量/调研窗口/误差范围；陈述性偏好只能当先行假设，必须用真实行为数据(购物篮、客流、官方统计)三角验证(MS AlphaWise、UBS、BoF-McKinsey)。
5. **跨行业传导链 (Cross-industry transmission map)** — 冲击(疗法/政策/价格战)沿价值链逐节点追踪：源头披露→行为数据→总量模型，每层标注来源与样本量，按替代风险给暴露排序(GLP-1)。
6. **事件证据纪律 (Event-evidence discipline)** — 临床/政策事件：写明病例数/样本量/中期vs最终/时间窗，再把临床终点翻译成市场规模、定价与采纳情景(Pfizer)。
7. **先分层再总合 (Segment before aggregate)** — 价格带/客户层级/城市线级/收入群体先分层：市场内部分化(Bain 仅1/3品牌增长、UBS 一线vs低线)才是真实信号，总合掩盖结构。
8. **政策打分的参数化 (Policy scoring parameterization)** — 法条→机制→参数化价格/预算效应→打分，静态价格效应与动态创新效应分开，假设全部显式可重跑(CBO)。
